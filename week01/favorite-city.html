My Favorite: Port Harcourt

Port Harcourt is a city that captures my imagination because of its incredible energy and fascinating history. Founded by the British in 1912, the city was originally built to export coal from the Enugu mines, but it truly came into its own when it became the heart of Nigeria's oil economy after the first crude oil shipment was exported through its port in 1958 . Now the capital of Rivers State, it is Nigeria's fifth most populous city and a bustling hub of commerce and industry . Nicknamed the "Garden City," it's known for its vibrant streets and the diverse mix of people and cultures that call it home .

What I find most compelling about Port Harcourt is its role as a dynamic industrial and cultural center. As the chief oil-refining city in Nigeria, it's a powerhouse where multinational firms like Shell and Chevron have a major presence . The Trans-Amadi Industrial Estate is a testament to its manufacturing might, producing everything from glass to steel . But there's more to this city than industry; it's also a place of learning and life, home to the University of Port Harcourt and other institutions, and a city that continues to grow and evolve as the economic heart of the Niger Delta .
